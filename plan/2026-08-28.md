## Block C — Notebook 02: your first confidence interval (~60 min)
5. Question: what's the mean body mass of Adelie penguins — with honest uncertainty?
6. Bootstrap it: 10,000 times, resample the Adelie masses with replacement (`rng.choice(masses, size=len(masses), replace=True)`) and store each mean.
7. `np.percentile(boot_means, [2.5, 97.5])` → your 95% CI. Histogram the bootstrap means, mark the interval.
8. The sentence that matters — write it in markdown, policy-brief style: "We estimate X g (95% CI: [Y, Z])..." then one more: what a 95% CI does and does NOT mean (yesterday's CLT is why this works).
